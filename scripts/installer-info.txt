Soundee for Windows 10/11 x64

Soundee is installed for your Windows account. Your profiles and settings are
preserved when upgrading or uninstalling.

System-wide EQ requires Equalizer APO 1.4.2. After installing Soundee, use its
Audio backend setup to install and attach Equalizer APO to your output device.
That separate step may require administrator permission and a restart.

Before uninstalling Soundee, disable its system EQ in the application. Soundee's
uninstaller preserves Equalizer APO, its configuration and configuration backups.
